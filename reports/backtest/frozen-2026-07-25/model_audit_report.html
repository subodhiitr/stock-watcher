Method and limitations

Training sessions: 2026-07-13, 2026-07-15, 2026-07-16, 2026-07-17. Later out-of-sample sessions: 2026-07-20, 2026-07-21. Snapshot and decision-journal coverage gates require start by 09:30 IST, end after 15:15 IST, at least 95% observed market minutes, and no gap above 15 minutes.

Recorded-decision replay limits the candidate universe to journaled entry intents. It can test whether a frozen rule set would accept and manage those recorded opportunities; it cannot reconstruct candidates that were never journaled. Results are model outputs, not a profit guarantee.
