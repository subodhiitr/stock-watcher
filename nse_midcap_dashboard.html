Yahoo Finance
Fetches prices directly from Yahoo Finance's public query1.finance.yahoo.com/v7 API. Real-time during market hours, 15-min delayed otherwise.

NSE Direct
Fetches from NSE India's official API (nseindia.com/api). Most accurate for Indian markets — requires proxy for CORS + session handling.
Proxy · No Key · Free

AI (Anthropic)
